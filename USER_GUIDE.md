# ORÉN Order & Inventory Tracker — User Guide

## Logging in
Go to the app's web address and enter the admin password.

## Adding a product
1. Click **Products** in the top menu.
2. Click **+ Add Product**.
3. Enter the name, size, price, and starting quantity, then click **Add Product**.

## Updating stock
1. On the Products page, click **Update stock** next to the item.
2. Enter the new quantity and click **Update**.

## Editing or removing a product
1. On the Products page, click **Edit** next to the item.
2. Change the name, size, or price and click **Save Changes**.
3. To remove a product, click **Delete Product**. If it has already been ordered, the system will block deletion to protect order history, set its quantity to 0 instead.

## Recording an order
1. Click **New Order** in the top menu.
2. Enter the customer's name.
3. Choose a product and quantity. Click **+ Add another item** for more products in the same order.
4. Set the payment status (paid or unpaid).
5. Click **Record Order**. Stock updates automatically.

## Viewing and updating orders
1. Click **Orders** to see all orders.
2. Click an order's number to see exactly what was bought.
3. Click **Update status** to change payment status or move the order through new → packed → delivered.

## Searching orders
On the Orders page, type a customer's name in the search box and click **Search**.

## Checking sales
Click **Sales Summary** to see total revenue (from paid orders only) and units sold per product.
